## Explain independently

In eight minutes, explain to a backend engineer how a short text input becomes
one new token. Include tokenization, vocabulary, embeddings, shapes, positions,
attention, residual/normalization/feed-forward blocks, logits, selection and the
next iteration. Explain where model parameters live and why normal inference
omits backpropagation.

In five minutes, connect this to Python workload choice, NumPy/PyTorch memory,
FP32 versus lower precision, host/device transfers and trustworthy timing. State
which parts you actually implemented and which remain conceptual.

## Review and revise

Use seven minutes to compare the explanation with selected sources. A reviewer
must first identify correct statements, incomplete statements, misconceptions,
unsupported claims and questions to answer next. Do not accept an immediate
rewritten “ideal answer” as evidence of your understanding.

Use the final five minutes to correct one weak explanation and record rubric
scores, assistance, missing hardware evidence and the next remediation task.
