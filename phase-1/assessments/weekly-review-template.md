# Weekly review

Assessment ID: weekly-review. Status: Not started.

## Week, dates and evidence

Module IDs, planned hours, actual hours, completed exercise links and source
IDs: To be recorded. An attended session is not a completed objective.

## Recognition

List five key distinctions from the week. Mark which you can identify without
notes.

## Explanation

Explain one concept in your own words. Preserve the original explanation, then
check it against the source. Record incomplete statements and misconceptions
separately.

## Application

Solve a fresh variant of an exercise, including shape or resource predictions.
Link code/calculations and correctness checks; record any assistance.

## Diagnosis

Describe one failure, two plausible causes and the smallest measurement that
would distinguish them. If using a hypothetical failure, label it hypothetical.

## Topic coverage and rubric

For each module objective, record recognition/explanation/application/diagnosis
scores using the [rubric](rubric.md), relevant evidence, and unresolved gaps.
Recognition-only topics do not require advanced proofs or algorithms.

## Prediction versus observation

What differed from your prediction? Which source helped explain the difference?
Do not insert measurements that were not collected.

## Blockers, catch-up and next week

Record missed work, hardware limitations, privacy review and a specific retry
plan. Keep planned and completed work distinct. Status remains In progress until
reviewed.
