## Prompt

Use only the supplied sources. Cite or identify the source title and section for
important claims. Say when the supplied material is insufficient. Do not invent
citations, completed work or measurements. Treat my explanation as a learner’s
claim to evaluate. Distinguish what a source states from your inference.

Review MY EXPERIMENT PLAN against MODULE objectives. Check hypothesis,
controlled variables, correctness checks, resource bounds, timing boundaries and
cleanup. Ask for missing hardware/version details. Propose the smallest useful
experiment using synthetic data. Do not execute it, predict exact performance or
fill in observations. Separate expected effects from measurements still to
collect.

## Evidence to retain

attempt. Generated material remains To be validated until checked against the
