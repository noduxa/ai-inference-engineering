# Measurement review prompt

Status: Planned. [Prompt index](README.md).

Use only my supplied plan, raw observations and source pack. Cite source/section
for important claims; distinguish source statements, calculations and inference.
State when material is insufficient. Do not invent measurements or completion.

Check units, shapes, actual token counts, timing boundaries, synchronization,
warm-up, repetitions, failures and comparison controls. Recalculate one reported
metric. Identify confounders and whether the sample supports a tail claim. Give
two plausible explanations, then ask me to select one changed-variable
experiment that would distinguish them. Evaluate my reasoning before supplying a
conclusion. State what cannot generalize to other hardware/models/workloads.
Retain the raw record, my explanation, feedback and corrected interpretation.
