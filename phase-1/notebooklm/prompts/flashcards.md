## Before use

Replace placeholders with the module, source IDs, question and your own attempt.
Verify that the selected import batch actually contains the needed sections.

## Prompt

Use only the supplied sources. Cite or identify the source title and section for
important claims. Say when the supplied material is insufficient. Do not invent
citations, completed work or measurements. Treat my explanation as a learner’s
claim to evaluate. Distinguish what a source states from your inference.

Create ten cards for MODULE: three distinctions, three small calculations or
code predictions, two failure diagnoses and two counterexamples. Show questions
separately from answers. Each answer needs a source/section and its assumptions.
Avoid cards whose answer is just a buzzword. Flag any card not answerable from
this import batch.

## Evidence to retain

Save your original attempt, feedback, checked citations and your corrected
attempt. Generated material remains To be validated until checked against the
sources.
