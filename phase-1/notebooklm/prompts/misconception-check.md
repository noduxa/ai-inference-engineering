## Prompt

Use only the supplied sources. Cite or identify the source title and section for
important claims. Say when the supplied material is insufficient. Do not invent
citations, completed work or measurements. Treat my explanation as a learner’s
claim to evaluate. Distinguish what a source states from your inference.

Evaluate MY CLAIMS one at a time. Classify each as supported, incomplete,
contradicted or not covered. Identify a source section and ask a probing
question before explaining the correction. Use a minimal counterexample where
the supplied sources permit it. Do not accept confident wording as evidence.

## Evidence to retain

Save your original attempt, feedback, checked citations and your corrected
attempt. Generated material remains To be validated until checked against the
sources.
