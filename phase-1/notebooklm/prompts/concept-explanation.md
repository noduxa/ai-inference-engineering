## Prompt

Use only the supplied sources. Cite or identify the source title and section for
important claims. Say when the supplied material is insufficient. Do not invent
citations, completed work or measurements. Treat my explanation as a learner’s
claim to evaluate. Distinguish what a source states from your inference.

Explain CONCEPT using the supplied section, then ask me to predict a tiny code,
shape or numerical example before revealing your reasoning. Compare it with
NEARBY CONCEPT to expose the boundary between them. Separate definition,
implementation detail and analogy. Ask what prerequisite is unclear before
adding more detail.
