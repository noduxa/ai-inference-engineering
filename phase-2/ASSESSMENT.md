# Phase 2 assessment and exit gate

Status: Not started. [Assessment files](assessments/README.md) ·
[Rubric](assessments/rubric.md)

Assess recognition, explanation, application and early diagnosis. Require a
complete request trace, clear prefill/decode explanation, assumption-based model
and KV calculations, fair benchmark design, interpretation of actual
measurements, a controlled performance investigation and an explicit account of
unknowns.

The final assessment occupies 3 hours inside the 60-hour plan. Experiments are
completed beforehand. Use fresh variants, retain unaided answers and disclose
assistance. Apply the [Phase 3 review](assessments/phase-3-readiness-review.md)
only after reviewing evidence; it authorizes further study, not expert status.
